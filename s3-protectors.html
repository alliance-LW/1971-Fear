What to Know

  • Protector’s Field unlocks and supports Desert Protectors.
  • Level 10 is a commonly recommended early stopping point because later upgrades compete for important seasonal resources.
  • Reserve Protectors for valuable seasonal objectives rather than routine zombies or low-value fighting.
  • The Altar later improves Protector stats after the Curse Research Lab reaches its required progression.
FEAR recommendation: conserve Protectors for alliance objectives where their loss produces meaningful season value.
