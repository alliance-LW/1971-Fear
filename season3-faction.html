Faction Duel Playbook

War-room briefing

Confirm the faction matchup, objectives, battle window, and the locations your leadership wants contested. Publish the plan before the opening push.

Rally and defense assignments

Name rally leads, reinforcement teams, scouts, and reserve squads. Keep key positions defended while maintaining a response force.

Attack timing

Scout enemy positions and synchronize arrivals. Concentrate force on priority targets rather than scattering squads across unrelated fights.

After-action reporting

Share battle results, casualties, and changes in enemy positioning with FEAR leadership so the next wave can be adjusted.
