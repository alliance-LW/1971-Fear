BATTLEFIELD DISCIPLINE

COMBAT

Combat tips for protecting troops, choosing fights and supporting FEAR as a coordinated alliance.

BEFORE ATTACKING

Scout Before You Attack

A standard scout report provides critical tactical intel before you commit troops:

  • Wall Strength & Defenses: Shows wall integrity and stationed defensive units.
  • Hero Lineup: Reveals the enemy's heroes, star levels, equipment, and exclusive weapons. Use this to identify their primary troop type (Tank, Aircraft, or Missile).
  • Available Resources: Displays plunderable loot (such as coins, steel, and food) to see if the target is worth hitting for resources.
  • Battle Reports vs. Scout Reports: If a standard scout report is blocked or hidden, you can send a single-troop test march with War Frenzy active to pull a light battle report.
LAST WAR TIP

Morale Matters

Higher troop morale can increase damage relative to an opponent. Squad size, troop level and relevant technology can influence combat performance, so troop quality matters alongside hero power.

TEAM PLAY

Rallies & Reinforcement

Use rallies for coordinated objectives and reinforce teammates when leadership calls for it. Keep marches available when an alliance objective is imminent.

DEFENSE

Protect Your Base

Use shields when you cannot actively defend during dangerous PvP windows. Watch hospital load and healing resources; avoid turning one bad fight into repeated troop losses.

RECOMMENDED

FEAR Recommendation

Fight for an objective: points, territory, defense or a rally target. Communicate before teleporting deep into enemy territory and keep a safe exit plan.

AVOID

Do Not Feed Points

Do not repeatedly hit a stronger target, attack into obvious reinforcement traps or stay unshielded while offline during major PvP periods.
