FEAR ALLIANCE PLAY

TEAM

FEAR grows faster when members coordinate. This page defines the habits that make an alliance stronger than the sum of its individual accounts.

COMMUNICATION

Follow Calls

Watch alliance announcements/chat for rally targets, territory instructions, event times, shield reminders and priority objectives.

SUPPORT

Help Each Other

Use Alliance Help, donate to Alliance Tech, join rallies and reinforce teammates when appropriate. Alliance systems reward active cooperation.

EVENTS

Show Up Together

Individual power helps; coordinated attendance wins objectives. Save marches and stamina for scheduled alliance events when leadership asks.

LAST WAR TIP

Coordinate VS

VS is an alliance score. Saving the correct resources for each themed day helps both your personal rewards and the alliance result.

RECOMMENDED

FEAR Recommendation

Ask before making a move that can affect the whole alliance—major PvP attacks, territory actions or cross-warzone engagements. Share useful battle reports and lessons.

AVOID

Team Killers

Avoid ignoring rally instructions, starting unnecessary conflicts, occupying marches needed for an objective, or giving opponents easy points while offline.
