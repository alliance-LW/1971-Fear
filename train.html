FEAR TRAIN PROCESS

Boarding Requirements

  • Meet at least 30,000 weekly Alliance Tech Donations.
  • Donate 3 Train Tickets when boarding.
SCHEDULE

How the Train Roster Is Selected

  • Weekly Train schedules are posted on Sundays.
  • Eligible members are ranked using 40% VS performance, 30% Alliance Tech Donations and 30% random selection.
  • To give everyone a fair opportunity at Conductor/VIP, a member who was selected for Conductor or VIP the previous week is not eligible for either position the following week.
  • The generator should only randomize among members who have first met the eligibility requirements; randomness does not override eligibility.
  • Train assignments should ideally be completed 10–15 minutes after server reset.
CONDUCTOR / VIP

Availability Requirements

  • Be online 30 minutes before reset.
  • Remain online after reset until both Conductor and VIP are assigned and the Train is Gold.
  • A selected member must respond/confirm availability before assignment.
  • If the selected member is unresponsive, the position passes to the next eligible alternate.
  • Detailed instructions will be sent to the selected Conductor and VIP.
AUTOMATION LOGIC

How the Generator Will Apply the Rules

  1. Load the current FEAR roster.
  2. Remove members with fewer than 30,000 weekly Alliance Tech Donations.
  3. Remove anyone who held Conductor or VIP during the previous week.
  4. Score each eligible member using the FEAR formula: 40% weekly VS performance + 30% weekly Alliance Tech Donations + 30% random.
  5. Normalize VS performance and Tech Donations within the eligible pool before applying their weights, so the different raw point scales do not distort the 40/30/30 formula.
  6. Generate a fresh random value for each eligible member for the 30% random component, giving qualifying members a meaningful fair-chance factor.
  7. Generate primary selections plus alternates for Conductor and VIP.
  8. Wait for the required member confirmation before the live assignment. If there is no response, move to the next alternate.
  9. Record the final assignments in Train history for the next week's cooldown check.

Manual checkpoint: Online status, the member's response, the 3-ticket boarding donation and confirmation that the Train reached Gold require live confirmation unless a future data source exposes those events.
