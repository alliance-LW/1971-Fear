THE FEAR CODE

ALLIANCE RULES

Starter rules for FEAR. Leadership can replace these with the exact Server 1971 policies.

01

Respect the Team

Keep chat constructive and treat alliance members with respect.

02

Follow War Calls

During coordinated events, leadership markers and instructions take priority over solo plays.

03

Stay Active

Participate consistently and communicate planned absences when possible.

04

Help Members Grow

Share useful information and help teammates understand alliance strategy.

05

No Point Feeding

Avoid unnecessary attacks or exposed marches when leadership calls for restraint.

06

Ask When Unsure

Before a major move that could affect the alliance, ask an R4/R5 if you are uncertain.
