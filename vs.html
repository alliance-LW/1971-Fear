ALLIANCE DUEL

VS

Day-by-day Alliance Duel planning for FEAR. Match your saved resources to the scoring category instead of spending randomly.

DAY 1

Radar Training

RADAR TRAINING Radar Tasks - Complete your tasks ON sunday so that it has a red dot, but DON'T COLLECT it until Monday. Resource Gathering - If your online before reset on Sunday send your squads to resource tiles for boost in points. (Make sure they will finish after server reset) Use Drone Parts and Data

DAY 2

Base Expansion

BASE EXPANSION Preparations on Mondays Building Upgrages - you can stack upgrades from buildings in your base without collecting the "giftboxes" and collect them on Tuesday. And use building speed ups for points. UR trucks - Legendary trucks, (you can roll the dice) UR secret tasks - Legendary

DAY 3

Age of Science

AGE OF SCIENCE Preparations on Tuesday Tech power - Check the timer for reset, start research in Tech Center that lines up with that time, so that it is ready to collect on wedneday. Radar Tasks - Complete your tasks ON Tuesday so that it has a red dot, but DON'T COLLECT it until Wednesday. Use research speed ups Use Valor Badges (alliance duel in research) Upgrade any Drone Components!

DAY 4

Train Heroes

HERO INITIATIVE Collect these items through out the week ! Hero EXP Use Tickets to recruit in Tavern Use Shards Skill Medals.

DAY 5

Total Mobilization

Preparations on ThursdayRadar Tasks - Complete your tasks ON Thursday so that it has a red dot, but DON'T COLLECT it until Friday. Training Troops Constuction Speedups Increase Building power Increase Tech Power

DAY 6

Enemy Buster

Legendary trucks, (you can roll the dice) UR secret tasks - Legendary Constuction Speedups Increase Building power Increase Tech Power Healing Speed ups Training Speedups Killing rival troops

LAST WAR TIP

Stack Your Points

The best VS habit is planning several days ahead. Start long construction/research so completion lands on the correct day, and hold qualifying consumables until their scoring day.

RECOMMENDED

FEAR Recommendation

Get your daily chests first, then decide whether pushing higher is worth the resources. During Enemy Buster, follow alliance calls instead of chasing solo points that expose teammates.
