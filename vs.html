DAY 0

Rest Day

🛠️ Prep for Tomorrow — Day 1: Radar Training

  • 📡 Let your Radar Tasks stack up. Complete the task itself—start the dig, gather the resources, help allies, etc.—but do not collect it from the Radar screen. If you need some tasks for daily chests, collect only the minimum required.
  • 🚶 Send troops out to gather. Time long gathering marches so they return after Day 1 begins.
  • 🚁 Save Drone Parts, Drone Battle Data and Drone Skill Chip Chests for Radar Training.
  • 🔥 Make sure you have stamina available for Day 1 scoring and Drone Boost opportunities.

🏁 Maximize Points with Arms Race

🪖 Unit Progression: Train troops and use Training Speed-ups to begin refilling your Drill Grounds after Enemy Buster.

🧪 Tech Research: Complete useful Tech Research and use Research Speed-ups if you need Arms Race points, while keeping tomorrow's Day 1 resources protected.

🚁 Drone Boost: Use stamina to reach your Arms Race chest target. Avoid spending Drone Battle Data or Drone Parts unless necessary because those resources score on Day 1.

🦸 Hero Advancement: Use the minimum Elite Recruitment Tickets or Hero EXP needed for your Arms Race target. Save excess hero resources for the next Train Heroes day.

🎁 City Building: Open only the building presents or use only the Construction Speed-ups needed for Arms Race. Avoid unnecessary spending.

🪖 Unit Progression: If Unit Progression returns, continue troop training and use Training Speed-ups as needed.

FEAR Day 0 rule: Rest Day is a setup day, not a waste-your-resources day. Stack Radar Tasks, send long gathers before reset, protect drone resources for Day 1, and use Arms Race only where it helps without hurting the coming VS week.
DAY 1

Radar Training

🎯 What to Do

  • Complete your saved Radar Tasks. On Sunday, complete them until they show a red dot, but do not collect them until Day 1 begins.
  • 🚁 Level up your drone using Drone Parts and Drone Battle Data.
  • 🔥 Use Stamina on attacks, rallies and radar missions.
  • 🦸 Level up heroes using Hero EXP.
  • 🚶 Send troops out gathering Food, Iron and Coins. Starting long gathers before reset can help them return during Day 1.
  • 💼 Open your saved Drone Skill Chip Chests.

💛 Prep for Tomorrow — Day 2: Base Expansion

  • 🎁 Leave completed building upgrades as presents/gift boxes to collect on Day 2.
  • Save your Construction Speed-ups.
  • Start medium or long building upgrades that can finish on Day 2.
  • Save Survivor Recruitment Cards.
  • Be ready to refresh Trade Trucks and Secret Tasks for Legendary/UR quality on Day 2.

🏁 Maximize Points with Arms Race

🧪 Tech Research: If you have long research running, use only the tech speed-ups needed to reach your Arms Race chests. Avoid overspending resources meant for later VS days.

🚁 Drone Boost: This is the best phase for Day 1 drone work. Use Drone Combat Data, Drone Parts and stamina here so the same actions can contribute to both VS and Arms Race.

🦸 Hero Advancement: If using Hero EXP on Day 1, time it with Hero Advancement and use only what you need for your target chests.

🎁 City Building: Use the minimum building completions or speed-ups needed for Arms Race if you participate, while preserving the bulk of construction resources for Day 2.

🪖 Unit Progression: Train or speed up only enough troops to reach your Arms Race target; preserve larger training pushes for the appropriate VS scoring day.

FEAR Day 1 rule: Double-dip whenever possible. Match Drone + Stamina with Drone Boost and Hero EXP with Hero Advancement, while saving construction resources for Day 2.
DAY 2

Base Expansion

🏗️ What to Do

  • 🚚 Send out Legendary/Gold UR Trade Trucks.
  • 🕵️ Perform Legendary/Gold Secret Tasks.
  • 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Recruit Survivors using the cards you saved.
  • 🏗️ Use Construction Speed-ups to increase building power and score VS points.
  • 🎁 Unwrap the completed building presents/gift boxes you saved from Day 1 to complete their upgrades and collect the power.

💗 Prep for Tomorrow — Day 3: Age of Science

  • Do not collect completed Radar Tasks. Complete them and let them build up for Day 3. If you must collect some for daily rewards, collect only what you need.
  • Start medium or long Tech Research so it can finish during Day 3.
  • Save your Research Speed-ups.
  • Save Valor Badges for Alliance Duel research.
  • Save Drone Component Chests for Day 3.

🏁 Maximize Points with Arms Race

🚁 Drone Boost: Use stamina during this phase. Save Drone Components for Day 3 rather than opening them on Day 2.

🦸 Hero Advancement: This is a lighter Day 2 overlap. Use Hero Recruit Tickets or a small amount of Hero EXP only if needed to reach your Arms Race chest target.

🎁 City Building: This is the major Day 2 overlap. Open your saved building presents, finish upgrades and use Construction Speed-ups. Include season-specific buildings when they provide useful building-power gains.

🪖 Unit Progression: Train, promote and use Training Speed-ups only as needed for your Arms Race chest target.

🧪 Tech Research: Use only enough research speed-ups for the Arms Race reward you want. Consider saving the bulk of them for Day 3, when research directly scores VS points.

🚁 Drone Boost: If Drone Boost appears again, stamina is a low-conflict way to earn Arms Race points without spending tomorrow's research resources.

FEAR Day 2 rule: Push building power today. Double-dip construction with City Building Arms Race, but protect Radar Tasks, Research Speed-ups, Valor Badges and Drone Components for Day 3.
DAY 3

Age of Science

🔬 What to Do

  • 📦 Open saved Drone Component Chests. If your alliance is planning a late push, consider holding some until closer to reset.
  • 🎓 Complete research that uses Valor Badges. Prioritize research that advances your combat goals; Special Forces is especially important for commanders working toward higher-tier troops.
  • 📡 Collect the Radar Tasks you completed and saved from Day 2.
  • 🔷 Use Research Speed-ups and complete research to increase Tech Power and score VS points.

💗 Prep for Tomorrow — Day 4: Train Heroes

  • Save your Hero Shards.
  • Save your Elite/Hero Recruit Tickets.
  • Save your Skill Medals.
  • Save your Legendary Hero Exclusive Weapon Shards.
  • Save your Hero EXP.

🏁 Maximize Points with Arms Race

🦸 Hero Advancement: Keep this light because Day 4 is the main hero day. If you need Arms Race chests, use only the minimum recruit tickets or Hero EXP necessary, then save the rest for after reset.

🎁 City Building: Use only the minimum saved building presents or Construction Speed-ups needed for your Arms Race target. Your main Day 3 resources should stay focused on science.

🪖 Unit Progression: Train or promote troops only as needed to reach your Arms Race chest target.

🧪 Tech Research: This is the best Day 3 Arms Race overlap. Time Research Speed-ups during Tech Research so the same research can score in both VS and Arms Race. Complete as much useful research as your resources allow, especially Valor Badge research.

🚁 Drone Boost: Use stamina here when possible. Saved Radar Tasks can consume stamina, so timing them with Drone Boost can add another scoring overlap. Otherwise, use stamina on rallies or zombies. Save actual drone level upgrades for the next Day 1 Radar Training unless you have a specific reason to use them now.

🦸 Hero Advancement: If this phase returns later in the day, continue protecting Hero EXP, shards, Skill Medals and recruit tickets for Day 4. Spend only what is needed for your Arms Race goal.

FEAR Day 3 rule: Research is the priority. Double-dip Research Speed-ups with Tech Research Arms Race, collect your saved Radar Tasks, use Valor Badges strategically, and protect hero resources for Day 4.
DAY 4

Train Heroes

🦸 What to Do

  • 🌟 Open your saved Hero Choice Chests and use the Hero Shards you have saved for the week. Ask alliance chat if you want advice before committing valuable universal shards.
  • 🏅 Level up hero skills using Skill Medals.
  • ⚔️ Use saved Legendary Hero Exclusive Weapon Shards on eligible heroes/exclusive weapons when the upgrade fits your build.
  • 🔷 Level up heroes using your saved Hero EXP.
  • 🟡 Use Legendary/Elite Recruitment Tickets for hero recruitment. Save special or season-specific tickets when they are more valuable for a future event.

💜 Prep for Tomorrow — Day 5: Total Mobilization

  • 📡 Complete Radar Tasks but do not collect them from the radar screen. Let them stack for Day 5.
  • 🎁 Leave some completed building upgrades sitting as presents/gift boxes.
  • 🧪 Have useful Tech Research timed to finish after Day 5 reset.
  • 🪖 Be ready to train or promote a large number of troops.
  • ⏱️ Save Construction, Research and Training Speed-ups.

🏁 Maximize Points with Arms Race

🎁 City Building: Use only the minimum building presents or Construction Speed-ups needed for your Arms Race chests. Save as much construction power as possible for Day 5.

🪖 Unit Progression: Train or promote only enough troops to reach your Arms Race target. Day 5 is a better day for a large troop push.

🧪 Tech Research: Use the minimum Research Speed-ups needed for Arms Race. Preserve the bulk of them for Total Mobilization.

🚁 Drone Boost: Stamina is a good low-conflict resource today. Use enough on rallies, zombies or other stamina activities to reach your target chests.

🦸 Hero Advancement: This is the major Day 4 Arms Race overlap. Time your Legendary Recruitment Tickets and Hero EXP during Hero Advancement so the same spending produces both VS and Arms Race points. Hero Shards and Skill Medals can also be used for Day 4 VS scoring where applicable.

🎁 City Building: If this phase returns later, continue using only what you need for Arms Race and protect remaining building completions and Construction Speed-ups for Day 5.

FEAR Day 4 rule: Today is hero day. Double-dip recruitment and Hero EXP with Hero Advancement, make your important shard/skill upgrades, then stop spending construction, research and training resources so they are ready for Day 5.
DAY 5

Total Mobilization

⚡ What to Do

  • 📡 Collect all the Radar Tasks you completed and saved from Day 4.
  • 🎁 Open your saved building presents/gift boxes. Use Construction Speed-ups on current or new building projects and complete useful upgrades.
  • 🧪 Use Research Speed-ups on useful research and complete it to gain Tech Power.
  • 🪖 Train and promote troops. Use Training Speed-ups and keep your Drill Grounds working.

💙 Prep for Tomorrow — Day 6: Enemy Buster

  • 🛡️ Stock up on Shields. If you will be offline or are not actively participating in combat, shield your base according to FEAR's Enemy Buster plan.
  • ⏱️ Stock up on Speed-ups. Day 6 can award points for using multiple types of speed-ups, depending on the current VS rules.
  • 🚚 Be ready to refresh and send Legendary/Gold Trade Trucks and complete Legendary/Gold Secret Tasks. These provide a non-combat scoring option.
  • ⚔️ Watch alliance chat and markers before attacking. Day 6 combat should follow alliance instructions.

🏁 Maximize Points with Arms Race

🦸 Hero Advancement: Keep hero spending light. Use only the recruitment tickets or Hero EXP needed to reach your Arms Race target; Day 4 was the primary hero-spending day.

🎁 City Building: This is a major Day 5 overlap. Open saved building presents, complete upgrades and use Construction Speed-ups while City Building is active to score in both VS and Arms Race.

🪖 Unit Progression: Another major Day 5 overlap. Fill your Drill Grounds, train/promote troops and use Training Speed-ups during this block.

🧪 Tech Research: Complete research and use Research Speed-ups during this phase for another strong double-dip. Consider preserving Valor Badges for the next Age of Science unless spending them now serves an important progression goal.

🚁 Drone Boost: Use only the stamina needed for your Arms Race target. Save actual drone upgrade resources for the next Day 1 Radar Training.

🦸 Hero Advancement: If it returns later, continue spending only the minimum needed for Arms Race chests.

FEAR Day 5 rule: Total Mobilization is the broad spending day. Double-dip Construction, Research and Training Speed-ups with their matching Arms Race phases, collect saved Radar Tasks, then prepare your shield and Day 6 scoring resources before reset.
DAY 6

Enemy Buster

⚔️ What to Do

  • 🛡️ SHIELD IF YOU ARE NOT ACTIVELY FIGHTING. If you are offline or not participating in an alliance-approved activity that requires you to be unshielded, keep a shield on.
  • 💣 Defeat troops from the VS opponent when participating in coordinated FEAR combat.
  • 🩸 Enemy troop kills can score points. Prioritize useful, coordinated fights rather than attacking blindly.
  • 💥 Avoid deliberately sacrificing your own troops just to generate points. Healing and troop replacement can be expensive.
  • ⏩ Use qualifying Speed-ups. Day 6 scoring can reward speed-up use rather than simply collecting completed upgrades, so check the current event rules before spending.
  • 🟡 Send Legendary/Gold UR Trade Trucks and perform Legendary/Gold Secret Tasks for non-combat scoring opportunities.

🏁 Maximize Points with Arms Race

🎁 City Building: If you still have Construction Speed-ups after Day 5, use them during City Building to combine Arms Race progress with qualifying Day 6 speed-up scoring.

🪖 Unit Progression: Use Training Speed-ups during this phase when they also qualify for Day 6 points. Keep enough troops available for alliance combat and defense.

🧪 Tech Research: Use Research Speed-ups on useful combat-related research. Unit, Hero and Garage research can strengthen your squads without necessarily consuming Valor Badges that may be better saved for Age of Science.

🚁 Drone Boost: Use stamina on useful activities such as rallies, zombies or event objectives. Actual drone upgrade resources are generally better saved for Day 1 Radar Training.

🦸 Hero Advancement: Use only the recruitment tickets or Hero EXP needed for your Arms Race chest target. Save excess hero resources for the next Day 4.

🎁 City Building: If City Building returns later, Construction Speed-ups can continue to double-dip with Day 6 speed-up scoring when the current event rules allow it.

FEAR Day 6 rule: Protect the alliance first. Stay shielded when you are not actively participating, follow FEAR combat calls, use Gold Trucks and Legendary Tasks for safer points, and time Speed-ups with matching Arms Race phases whenever possible.
LAST WAR TIP

Stack Your Points

The best VS habit is planning several days ahead. Start long construction/research so completion lands on the correct day, and hold qualifying consumables until their scoring day.

RECOMMENDED

FEAR Recommendation

Get your daily chests first, then decide whether pushing higher is worth the resources. During Enemy Buster, follow alliance calls instead of chasing solo points that expose teammates.
