COMMANDER INTEL

Quick-reference priorities for alliance members.

01DAILY

Alliance First

Donate to alliance tech, help teammates, join rallies, read markers and keep an eye on alliance chat before major moves.

02VS

Score With Purpose

Save key upgrades, speedups and resources for the matching Alliance Duel day whenever possible. Coordinate big pushes with leadership.

03COMBAT

Fight Together

Solo power matters. Coordinated power wins. Follow rally calls, reinforce when asked and avoid feeding points during protected strategies.

04GROWTH

Build Efficiently

Keep research, construction and training moving. Prioritize upgrades that improve your core squads and long-term combat output.

05SQUADS

Know Your Heroes

Build around complementary hero roles and unit types. Last War includes Tank, Aircraft and Missile heroes with attack, defense and support roles.

06TEAM

Communicate

If you're going to miss an important event, say so. Clear communication helps leadership plan rallies, defenses and territory moves.

THE FEAR CODE

A strong alliance is more than power.

01

Respect the Team

Keep alliance chat constructive. Disagreements happen; personal attacks don't help us win.

02

Follow War Calls

During coordinated events, leadership markers and instructions take priority over solo plays.

03

Stay Active

Participation keeps the alliance strong. Communicate planned absences when possible.

04

Help Members Grow

Share knowledge, answer questions and help newer commanders become useful teammates faster.
