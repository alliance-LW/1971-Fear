SEASON 3: GOLDEN KINGDOM

A FEAR-focused guide to Curse Resistance, Spice, seasonal troops and disciplined alliance warfare in the Golden Kingdom.

FEAR Season 3 Playbook

Golden Kingdom: Season 3 introduces a desert/Egyptian theme with Spice competition, Mithril and Sacred Water, Curse Resistance, Desert Protectors, Sandworm content and alliance territory warfare.
OPENING

Build With a Plan

Current community guides emphasize early seasonal-building order. A commonly cited opening is Protector's Field to level 10, then prioritizing the Curse Research Lab and keeping Blessing Fountains progressing evenly.

RESISTANCE

Curse Resistance Matters

Do not judge a rally only by raw squad power. Curse Resistance gates seasonal content, and community guides report that the rally leader's resistance is especially important for group attacks.

SPICE

Play for the Season Resource

Spice is a major alliance-ranking objective. Territory ownership and season warfare should be evaluated by how they help FEAR's season position, not just individual kill counts.

PROTECTORS

Don't Waste Special Troops

Desert Protectors are a seasonal resource. Community references recommend reserving them for valuable seasonal objectives rather than routine zombies or low-value PvP.

SANDWORMS

Farm Seasonal Content

Watch the season event schedule for Sandworm Crisis and other limited opportunities. Coordinate stronger targets with FEAR rather than leaving rewards on the table.

R4/R5

One Alliance, One Plan

Territory, Spice Wars and other map objectives become much easier when marches land together. Check FEAR instructions before committing teleports or troops.

DO THIS

Season 3

  • Prioritize Curse Resistance progression.
  • Keep seasonal resource production active.
  • Save special troops for valuable objectives.
  • Join coordinated Spice/territory pushes.
  • Track individual season contribution.
DON'T

Avoid These

  • Do not over-invest blindly in one seasonal building.
  • Do not send weak-resistance rally leaders when a stronger one is available.
  • Do not burn Desert Protectors on routine targets.
  • Do not chase kills while FEAR is defending an objective.
  • Do not hoard expiring seasonal resources until the season ends.
DAILY

60-Second Checklist

  • ☐ Collect seasonal resources
  • ☐ Upgrade Curse Resistance path
  • ☐ Check FEAR territory orders
  • ☐ Complete seasonal event tasks
  • ☐ Preserve special troops
  • ☐ Keep normal squad/research growth active
