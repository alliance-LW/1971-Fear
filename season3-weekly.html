5 Key Points — Weekly Progression

  1. Complete daily seasonal tasks to avoid missing limited progression rewards.
  2. Prioritize Curse Resistance and other upgrades needed for the next objective.
  3. Plan seasonal resource spending rather than upgrading everything at once.
  4. Review alliance milestones and prepare squads for scheduled events.
  5. Check progress at the end of each week and adjust priorities for the next phase.
