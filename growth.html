BUILD SMARTER

GROWTH

Efficient account growth comes from concentrating scarce resources where they improve your main fighting force and unlock the next progression step.

BASE

HQ & Prerequisites

Work backward from your next HQ requirement. Upgrade prerequisite buildings with purpose rather than leveling everything evenly.

RESEARCH

Technology

Keep research active. Prioritize technology that supports your main squad, troop capability, economy and event efficiency based on your current bottleneck.

LAST WAR TIP

Alliance Help

The Alliance Support Hub lets alliance members reduce upgrade, research and healing time through help. Staying in an active alliance makes long timers easier to manage.

RESOURCES

Protect Scarce Materials

Coins, hero shards, skill medals, drone materials, gear ore and speed-ups become major bottlenecks. Avoid spreading premium materials across heroes you rarely use.

RECOMMENDED

FEAR Recommendation

Main squad first, then Squad 2. Keep builders/research working and align major completions with VS or other scoring events whenever practical.

AVOID

Power Without Purpose

Do not chase total power alone. Prefer upgrades that improve actual combat performance, unlock progression or earn useful event rewards.
