Spice Wars Playbook

Before the event

Check the active Spice Wars rules and scoring objectives in-game. Identify which activities award points and agree on priorities before members commit resources.

Resource management

Set aside the resources needed for the event, avoid unnecessary spending, and compare reward value before committing expensive upgrades or marches.

Alliance coordination

Use R4 target calls and event timing. Assign members to gathering, reinforcement, or combat duties based on the current objective.

During the event

Report progress and enemy activity, protect exposed squads, and avoid independent attacks that conflict with alliance plans.
