HEROES

FEAR hero reference for Last War: Survival. Browse the full 30-hero roster, compare hero roles and core skills, and focus your upgrades around the Exclusive Weapons that can change how UR heroes perform in battle.

EXCLUSIVE WEAPON GUIDE

Exclusive Weapon Milestones

Exclusive Weapons are hero-specific progression systems. A 5-star hero and 50 named weapon shards are generally required for the initial activation. After activation, weapon upgrades increase hero stats and unlock major effects at milestone levels. Universal shards can be used for upgrades after activation.

TANK

Kimberly

UR
Attack

High-output AOE Energy Damage carry. Build skills around her barrage damage and attack scaling.

SKILLS

  • Barrage Strike — Fires a volley of rockets at random enemies for Energy Damage. Higher skill tiers add more rockets and increase the damage, making this her main multi-target burst skill.
  • Energy Boost — Passively increases Kimberly's Energy Damage throughout the battle.
  • Super Sensing — Expertise that raises Kimberly's HP, Attack and Defense and improves her skill cooldown rate.

EXCLUSIVE WEAPON

Rocket Shadow

Adds Energy Amplification-style scaling and strengthens her AOE damage. A major Tank carry weapon.

TANK

Murphy

UR
Defense

Front-row defender focused on Physical Damage reduction and protecting the formation.

SKILLS

  • Cannon Fire — Basic cannon attack that deals Physical Damage to an enemy.
  • Ironclad Barrier — Temporarily increases Physical Damage Reduction for the front row; at higher tiers the protection can extend to the whole squad and last longer.
  • Stand Firm — Passively reduces damage taken by front-row units, strengthening the squad's frontline.
  • Super Sensing — Expertise that raises Murphy's HP, Attack and Defense and improves skill cooldown rate.

EXCLUSIVE WEAPON

Mitigation Master

Enhances damage mitigation for allies and greatly improves front-line durability.

TANK

Williams

UR
Defense

Defensive anchor built around damage reduction, defense buffs and front-row survival.

SKILLS

  • Cannon Fire — Basic cannon attack that deals Physical Damage.
  • Ironclad Barrier — Temporarily boosts Physical Damage Reduction for the front row, with stronger tiers extending duration and team coverage.
  • Defensive Passive — Improves frontline durability and helps Williams function as a defensive anchor in longer battles.

EXCLUSIVE WEAPON

Exclusive Weapon available

Strengthens Williams' defensive kit, including his defense buff and personal damage reduction.
