🚂 Conductor Instructions

Instructions for the selected Train Conductor when preparing the Gold Train and assigning the Special Guest (VIP).

Before Assigning

Be online 30 minutes prior to reset. We will need a response prior to assigning you as Conductor, after alliance events. If you are unresponsive, we will assign someone else as Conductor.

After Assigning

  1. Remain online after reset until the Train turns Gold — approximately 30 minutes to 1 hour.
  2. Turn the Train Gold using the refresh button at the bottom. Use 5 refreshes of 5 Contract Tickets. As people board, Contract Tickets will be donated to you. Collect the tickets to help reach the 25 tickets needed to make the Train Gold. The collection area is at the bottom right of the screen; click into it to collect.
  3. Check your squad formations located at the front of the Train and make adjustments as needed. Do NOT select “Guards Engage Enemies Randomly” at the bottom of formations.
  4. Train Value should be 50K or above. This may require a few additional refreshes.
  5. After the Gold Train is set, assign the VIP once leadership gives you the green light. The VIP/Special Guest icon is located on the red carpet. Assign the designated player as your Special Guest.
Important: Do not assign the Special Guest/VIP until leadership confirms the player and gives you the green light.
