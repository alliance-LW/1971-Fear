FREE-TO-PLAY FIELD GUIDE

A practical progression plan for FEAR commanders who want to get maximum value from free resources, event timing and alliance participation.

Grow Strong Without Spending

F2P rule: efficiency beats impulse. Concentrate limited materials into permanent progression, one strong primary squad and event timing that lets the same upgrade earn multiple rewards.
#1

Build One Main Squad

Avoid leveling every hero evenly. Put EXP, shards, skill medals and your best gear into the formation you actually fight with. Secondary squads can catch up later.

#2

Keep Permanent Growth Running

Builders and research queues should rarely be idle. Push HQ/Tech Center requirements and research that unlocks meaningful combat or progression rather than cosmetic power.

#3

Bank Before Events

Save speedups, shards and large upgrades for the matching Alliance Duel/VS or Arms Race windows when practical. One expenditure can then advance your account and score event rewards.

#4

Join an Active Alliance

Alliance helps, gifts, research, rallies and event rewards are a huge part of free progression. Donate and participate every day; inactive solo play gives away free value.

#5

Protect Rare Materials

Universal legendary shards, high-end blueprints, Valor Badges and similar scarce materials should have a plan before you spend them. Avoid upgrading something simply because the button is available.

#6

Use Stores Intentionally

Different stores use different currencies. Prioritize items that are difficult to farm and advance permanent power; compare the current store before buying routine resources you can gather elsewhere.

DAILY

F2P Daily Routine

  1. Collect free/daily rewards.
  2. Keep construction and research running.
  3. Donate/help alliance members.
  4. Use stamina on worthwhile PvE/event targets.
  5. Complete daily/event milestones you can reach efficiently.
  6. Gather when marches would otherwise sit idle.
  7. Check tomorrow's event before spending major items.
WEEKLY

F2P Weekly Routine

  • Plan upgrades around Alliance Duel/VS categories.
  • Review main squad: weakest gear/skill/star bottleneck first.
  • Spend store currencies with a target list.
  • Bank anything that has poor value this week.
  • Participate in FEAR alliance events for shared rewards.
RESOURCES

Don't Open Everything

Keep choice/resource chests unopened until you know what upgrade needs them. This preserves flexibility and can be especially useful when chest value or needs change with progression.

DO THIS

Efficient F2P

  • Focus your strongest march.
  • Stack upgrades with event scoring.
  • Stay in an active alliance.
  • Save scarce currencies for high-value items.
  • Gather and use free stamina consistently.
  • Plan before opening choice chests.
DON'T

Resource Traps

  • Do not spread shards across every hero.
  • Do not impulse-spend diamonds on routine speedups.
  • Do not chase spender-dominated rankings at any cost.
  • Do not let queues sit idle for long periods.
  • Do not burn rare blueprints/badges without a long-term target.
  • Do not skip alliance events because your power is lower.
FEAR TIP

Contribution > Ego

A strong F2P player can be extremely valuable by showing up, reinforcing, joining rallies, following markers and scoring consistently. Reliability creates more alliance value than one expensive march that misses the objective.
