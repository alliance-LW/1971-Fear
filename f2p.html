FREE-TO-PLAY GUIDE

A practical FEAR guide for commanders who want to progress efficiently while spending little or nothing. The key is consistency, focus and timing.

01

Never Waste Free Activity

Collect free shop items, complete daily tasks, use stamina intelligently, claim event rewards and keep construction, research and training queues active.

02

Focus Your Main Squad

Do not try to max everything. Concentrate scarce hero XP, shards, skill resources and gear materials on the squad that carries most of your combat power.

03

Save for Scoring Windows

When possible, hold speedups, recruitment tickets, upgrades and other valuable consumables until they also earn event or Alliance Duel points.

04

Alliance = Free Value

Join rallies, use alliance help, donate, participate in events and collect alliance rewards. An active alliance dramatically improves free progression.

05

Spend Currency Carefully

Avoid impulse purchases in rotating shops. Prioritize items that remove a meaningful progression bottleneck for your main squad or long-term research.

06

Protect Your Resources

Do not carry unnecessary exposed resources into risky combat periods. Scout situations carefully and follow FEAR instructions during server conflict.

07

Consistency Beats Bursts

A free player who logs in consistently, finishes events and plans upgrades can outperform poorly managed higher-spend accounts over time.

08

Ask Before Using Rare Items

If you are unsure about a rare currency, universal shard or major upgrade material, ask FEAR leadership before committing it.
