SEASON 2: POLAR WORLD

A practical FEAR checklist for surviving the cold, building the seasonal economy and staying ready for coordinated alliance objectives.

FEAR Season 2 Playbook

Core loop: Polar World adds cold/temperature pressure, seasonal resources and buildings. Titanium Alloy is central to upgrading the High-Heat Furnace; alliance coordination becomes increasingly important for territory and Rare Soil competition.
DAY 1

Start the Alloy Engine

  • Build the Titanium Alloy Factory immediately.
  • Prioritize Alloy production because Furnace growth depends on it.
  • Collect first-kill and map rewards that provide seasonal materials.
FURNACE

Heat Is Progress

  • Keep the High-Heat Furnace moving upward.
  • Plan fuel/heat before blizzards rather than reacting after they begin.
  • Do not burn seasonal materials on low-impact choices while Furnace upgrades are blocked.
FACTORIES

Unlock More Production

Community references report that additional free Alloy Factories unlock as the previous factory reaches level 15. That makes early factory progression a strong compounding play.

MAP

Follow FEAR Markers

Season 2 rewards organized territory expansion. Rally on called targets, reinforce priority positions and avoid scattering marches on low-value fights.

RARE SOIL

Think Alliance-First

Later-season competition centers heavily on alliance objectives and Rare Soil. Save combat power for coordinated windows and follow R4/R5 timing.

SQUAD

Keep One March Dangerous

Season systems do not replace normal progression. Keep your strongest squad, research and troop tier advancing while you build seasonal resistance.

DO THIS

Season 2

  • Build Alloy production early.
  • Keep Furnace upgrades moving.
  • Check alliance announcements before major pushes.
  • Bank enough resources for scheduled war windows.
  • Use first-kill opportunities for seasonal rewards.
DON'T

Avoid These

  • Do not ignore temperature preparation.
  • Do not solo objectives leadership is organizing.
  • Do not waste troops on meaningless fights before a push.
  • Do not let seasonal production sit idle.
  • Do not assume old season mechanics are unchanged.
DAILY

60-Second Checklist

  • ☐ Collect/upgrade Alloy production
  • ☐ Check Furnace and heat status
  • ☐ Complete seasonal first kills/tasks
  • ☐ Read FEAR markers/chat
  • ☐ Keep research/builders active
  • ☐ Save marches for called objectives
